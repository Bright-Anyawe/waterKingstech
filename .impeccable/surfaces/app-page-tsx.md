# Surface brief: WaterKingsTech shop (home, listings, product, hubs, corporate)

Scope: the whole public site. Visitor mode: Operate (marketplace browsing: search, filter, compare, contact) with Persuade moments (bundle banners, deal tags, corporate pitch). Code-led build (no image generation available).

Audience and job: Accra buyers on phones find a device or repair and open WhatsApp; corporate buyers request a bulk quote; agents learn the referral tiers. Proof on hand: the real shop location, the 3-month UK-used warranty, the free gift bundles, the commission tables. Constraints: no invented prices, turnaround times, reviews or terms; null renders an amber placeholder.

## Direction contract

THESIS: WaterKingsTech works like Jiji: a coloured search band, a category rail with live counts and a dense listing grid where every card ends in WhatsApp. It refuses the old dark cinematic brand hero and the centred-headline-over-equal-cards landing.

OWN-WORLD: Tidewater palette from the logo. Royal blue #0c6de2 to cyan #25c3f4 owns the search band and links. The ground is ice blue #eef4fa, cards are white with a 1px #d9e5f1 border and 12px radius, and prices are bold royal blue. Orange #ff7a00 is the one counter-accent (Hot Deals, the phone bundle). Deal tags are red SAVE, orange HOT DEAL and violet WEEKEND FLASH SALE. Gift chips are green and Buy buttons WhatsApp green. Montserrat ExtraBold, echoing the logo's wordmark, is the display face; Geist is the UI face.

STORY: Visitors understand within seconds that this is a real Circle Mall shop with deals and warranty. They search or tap a category, compare, then tap Buy on WhatsApp; corporate buyers request a quote.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A navy ticker strip with the bundle headliners. A white header holding the logo lockup, Retail | Corporate and an orange Hot Deals button. A blue band with "What are you looking for?", a category select plus a 56px search and popular chips, ending in the logo's wave curve (the signature move). Below it, the category rail and the two bundle banners begin above the fold at 1440x900.

FORM: Jiji.com.gh marketplace canon, pinned by the user (position 1). seed: none (direction pinned by user; no roll run)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Memorable moment
The wave-edged blue search band with the ticker running above it, then a grid of deal-tagged cards whose free-gift chip and green WhatsApp button make the next step obvious.

## Unresolved
Repair turnaround times, real stock and prices, shop photos, reviews, hours, installment and referral terms, exact map pin.
